# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

Zentrola welcomes respectful, constructive participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Zentrola 欢迎不同背景、身份、经验和观点的参与者进行尊重且有建设性的协作。

## Expected behavior / 应有行为

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Protect private information and never pressure anyone to disclose credentials or user data.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope, safety, and moderation.

- 针对观点和代码展开讨论，不针对个人特征。
- 提供具体、可执行的反馈，并善意接受纠正。
- 保护隐私，不要求任何人披露凭据或用户数据。
- 尊重维护者对范围、安全和社区管理作出的决定。

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private or sensitive information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

骚扰、威胁、歧视、蓄意破坏、曝光隐私以及公开敏感信息均不可接受。必要时，维护者可以编辑或删除内容、关闭讨论，或临时/永久限制参与。

Report conduct concerns privately to `longjianghu1982@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

行为准则问题请私下发送至 `longjianghu1982@gmail.com`。维护者将在实际可行范围内保密处理。本准则适用于项目仓库、讨论、活动及代表本项目参与的其他场景。
