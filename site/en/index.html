Connecting a model is easy. Governing its use takes more.

Once people, apps, and providers share the same setup, access, credentials, and spending need clear ownership.

Credentials spread everywhere

Real provider API keys get copied into laptops and app settings, making rotation and revocation difficult.

Zentrola issues separate keys to users and apps while keeping upstream credentials centrally.

Model access lacks boundaries

Teams need to know who can use each model and apply the same rules across different clients.

Grant logical models through Groups; enforce permissions and quotas at the gateway.

Costs have no clear owner

A provider total alone does not show which people, apps, or models drove the usage.

Attribute usage to users, apps, models, and resources, with cost details and monthly quotas.

One control plane between existing tools and models.

Keep the AI coding clients your developers know. Zentrola brings the management and model gateway together.
